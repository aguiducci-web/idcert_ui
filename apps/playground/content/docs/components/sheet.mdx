## Examples

### Sides

`SheetContent` accepts `side` of `top`, `right`, `bottom`, or `left`. Right is
the default and the right call for most edit drawers and settings panels. Use
`bottom` on mobile patterns where the sheet behaves like an action sheet, and
`left` when the sheet is acting as a navigation drawer.

<Example name="Sides" component="Sheet"><Sides /></Example>

### With a form

Sheet bodies are the canonical home for medium-length forms — long enough that
a Popover would feel cramped, short enough that a full page would be overkill.
Wire the submit button in `SheetFooter` to the form via the `form` attribute so
the layout can keep the form scrollable and the footer pinned.

<Example name="WithForm" component="Sheet"><WithForm /></Example>

### Controlled

Pass `open` and `onOpenChange` to control visibility from a parent. Use this
when the open state is derived from URL, a row selection, or another component
— for purely local triggers, leave Sheet uncontrolled and let `SheetTrigger`
handle it.

<Example name="Controlled" component="Sheet"><Controlled /></Example>

### Scrollable body

For long content (changelogs, audit logs, comment threads), keep
`SheetHeader`/`SheetFooter` outside the scroll region and give the body
`overflow-y-auto`. The sheet stays a fixed height; only the body scrolls so
the title and primary actions never leave the viewport.

<Example name="WideContent" component="Sheet"><WideContent /></Example>

## Accessibility

- Sheet renders as a modal dialog: focus is trapped inside `SheetContent` while
  open, `Esc` closes it, and focus returns to the trigger on close. Do not
  reimplement these — let the primitive own them.
- `aria-modal="true"` and `role="dialog"` are applied automatically on the
  popup. Do not add them yourself.
- A `SheetTitle` is required for an accessible name. If your design hides the
  visible title, keep `SheetTitle` in the tree and apply `sr-only` instead of
  removing it.
- Pair `SheetTitle` with a `SheetDescription` when the panel needs more context
  than the title gives — it is wired to `aria-describedby` automatically.
- Clicking the backdrop dismisses the sheet by default. For destructive forms
  with unsaved input, intercept `onOpenChange` and confirm before closing
  rather than disabling outside-click globally.
- The built-in close button has `aria-label="Close"`. Hide it with
  `showCloseButton={false}` only when you provide your own labelled close
  control inside the sheet.
